Why a calculator result can be misleading

A mathematically correct result can still be a poor real-world answer when an input is incomplete. Borrowing decisions can depend on lender-specific fees or repayment rules. Salary estimates can depend on tax codes, benefits and pension treatment. Property taxes can change with buyer status and transaction details.

Every WorthChex calculator therefore includes a limitations section. Read it before relying on the number, especially for tax, property, borrowing or investment-related decisions.

Use the calculator as a scenario builder.

Try your realistic case, then change one important assumption and compare the result. The comparison often tells you more than the headline figure.
