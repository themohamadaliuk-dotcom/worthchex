PRIVACY POLICY

How WorthChex handles information.

WorthChex is designed so that the figures you enter into its calculators are processed in your browser. The website does not require an account or bank details to use the calculators.

1. Calculator inputs

Calculator figures are processed locally in your browser for the purpose of producing the results shown on the page. WorthChex does not need those figures to operate an account for you.

2. Cookies, analytics and advertising

WorthChex may use third-party services such as Google services for advertising, consent management, security or website measurement. Those services may process information according to their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

3. Third-party services

Where third-party content or services are used, their own privacy policies and terms may also apply. WorthChex does not control the privacy practices of independent third parties.

4. Data security

WorthChex aims to minimise the personal information it asks visitors to provide. Calculator inputs are intended to remain in the browser rather than being submitted to a WorthChex account or customer database.

5. Children's privacy

WorthChex is a general-audience financial information website and is not directed specifically at children.

6. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the website, its services or applicable requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.

7. Important information

WorthChex provides financial calculators and educational information only. Calculator results are estimates and are not personalised financial advice.

8. Google advertising and cookies

WorthChex uses Google advertising services on pages where ads are enabled. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to deliver, measure and improve advertising. Depending on the settings used, advertising may be personalised based on a visitor's activity on WorthChex or elsewhere.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on a visitor's browser as part of ad serving. Visitors can manage or opt out of personalised advertising through Google's Ads settings. More information is available in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

9. Consent and UK privacy choices

Where advertising or analytics requires consent under applicable UK privacy rules, WorthChex will use an appropriate consent mechanism and respect the choices provided by visitors. Advertising preferences can also be changed through the controls provided by Google or the relevant consent service.

10. Contact and policy changes

This policy may be updated when WorthChex adds or changes services, advertising technology, analytics or legal requirements. The current version is published on this page.
