SALARY GUIDE

What is salary sacrifice?

Salary sacrifice is an arrangement where an employee gives up part of their gross salary in return for an employer-provided benefit. For some benefits, the sacrificed amount can reduce taxable pay and employee National Insurance, subject to the rules that apply.

Why it can change take-home pay

The effect depends on the benefit, the amount sacrificed and the payroll arrangement. A salary-sacrifice pension can reduce the pay used for Income Tax and, in many cases, employee National Insurance calculations. The exact payslip result depends on the scheme and circumstances.

Do not compare the sacrificed amount directly with the fall in take-home pay: tax and National Insurance savings can mean the net reduction is smaller than the gross contribution.
