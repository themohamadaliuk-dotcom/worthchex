MONEY GUIDES

Practical explanations behind the numbers.

WorthChex guides answer common questions in plain language and connect the explanation to a calculator where a visitor can test their own scenario. The guides are designed to explain assumptions, trade-offs and limitations rather than simply repeat a headline figure.

HOW TO USE THE GUIDES

Read the context before you trust the number.

Start with the question

Choose the guide that matches the decision you are trying to make: salary, savings, borrowing, property or household affordability.

Check the assumptions

Look for tax year, jurisdiction, interest-rate assumptions and other facts that can change a calculation.

Then test your numbers

Use the linked WorthChex calculator to move from the general explanation to a personal planning scenario.

For time-sensitive tax, property or VAT rules, the guides link to official GOV.UK sources. Rules can change, so check the current source before making an important decision.

HOW TO USE THE LIBRARY

Start with context, then put your own numbers into the tool.

The guides are intended to answer the question behind a calculation: what the number means, which assumptions change it, what a simple example looks like and where the calculator stops being representative of a real product or personal situation.

Broad guides

Start with household budgeting, loan costs, salary after tax or mortgage affordability when you need the concepts before the arithmetic.

Scenario guides

Use the focused examples for questions such as £40,000 salary take-home pay or a £300,000 property as worked illustrations rather than universal answers.

Verify changeable rules

Tax, property and VAT rules can change. Follow the official source linked on the relevant guide before making an important decision.

Editorial standard

WorthChex aims to publish fewer, more useful explanations rather than padding pages to meet a target word count. A guide should add context, examples, calculations, trade-offs or limitations that are useful even when the reader does not use the linked calculator.
