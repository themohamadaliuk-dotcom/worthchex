MORTGAGE GUIDE

Is overpaying your mortgage worth it?

Usually, yes, if you have no expensive debts and a cash buffer. On a £200,000 mortgage at 4.5% over 25 years, an extra £100 a month saves about £21,142 in interest and clears the loan 3 years 6 months early.

LUMP SUMS

What about a one-off payment?

Example: paying a £10,000 lump sum after 5 years, while keeping the same monthly payment, saves about £13,634 in interest and finishes the mortgage 1 year 9 months early.

Most lenders let you overpay up to 10% of the balance each year without a charge during a fixed or tracker deal. Go above that and you may pay an early repayment charge (ERC), often 1–5% of the excess. Check your mortgage offer, or wait until your deal ends, when overpayments are usually unlimited.

When you overpay, many lenders ask whether you want to shorten the term or lower the monthly payment. Shortening the term saves the most interest; lowering the payment gives you more breathing room each month.

OVERPAY OR SAVE?

Compare it with what your savings earn after tax.

Overpaying a 4.5% mortgage is like earning a guaranteed, tax-free 4.5% return. To beat it, a savings account would need to pay more than this after tax:

Your situationSavings rate needed to beat overpaying
Interest still within your Personal Savings Allowance, or in an ISA4.5%
Basic-rate taxpayer, allowance used up (20% tax)5.63%
Higher-rate taxpayer, allowance used up (40% tax)7.50%

The Personal Savings Allowance lets basic-rate taxpayers earn £1,000 of interest a year tax-free, and higher-rate taxpayers £500. Interest inside an ISA is always tax-free.

WHEN NOT TO OVERPAY

Do these first.

  1. Clear expensive debt. A credit card at 24.9% APR costs far more than a 4.5% mortgage. Every £100 there saves much more.
  2. Build an emergency fund. Money paid into a mortgage is hard to get back. Keep 3–6 months of essential costs in easy-access savings. See how much to keep.
  3. Take any free pension money. If your employer matches extra contributions, that match usually beats overpaying.
  4. Check the ERC. Stay within the 10% allowance unless the saving clearly outweighs the charge.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Mortgage overpayment FAQs

Is it better to overpay monthly or in a lump sum?

The earlier money goes in, the more it saves. A regular monthly overpayment from today usually saves more than saving up and paying a lump sum later, as long as you stay within your lender's limit.

Does overpaying reduce my monthly payment?

Only if you ask the lender to recalculate. Otherwise most lenders keep the payment the same and the mortgage simply ends sooner.

Does overpaying help when I remortgage?

Yes. A lower balance means a lower loan-to-value, which can qualify you for better rates at your next deal.

Can I get overpayments back?

Some flexible mortgages let you borrow back overpayments, but most do not. Treat overpaid money as locked away.
