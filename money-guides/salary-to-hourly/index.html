How do you convert an annual salary into an hourly rate?

A simple annual-to-hourly conversion divides annual pay by the number of paid working hours in a year. For take-home pay, use your estimated net salary instead of gross salary.

The simple formula

Gross hourly rate = gross annual salary ÷ annual working hours.

For a quick 40-hour-week example, 52 weeks gives 2,080 hours. £40,000 ÷ 2,080 is about £19.23 gross per hour. Your actual contracted hours, paid leave and unpaid weeks can make the real comparison different.

Gross versus take-home hourly pay

A gross hourly rate does not account for Income Tax, National Insurance, pension contributions or student-loan deductions. WorthChex can show an hourly equivalent after selected deductions as well.
