SALARY GUIDE

How much of your salary do you actually take home?

Your gross salary is the amount before deductions. Your take-home pay is what remains after Income Tax, National Insurance and any pension or student-loan deductions that apply.

What reduces your salary?

For a typical employee, Income Tax is based on taxable income after allowances. National Insurance is calculated using its own earnings thresholds. Pension contributions and student-loan repayments can reduce the amount you receive as well.

For 2026/27 the standard Personal Allowance is £12,570. In England, Wales and Northern Ireland the main Income Tax rates are 20%, 40% and 45%, while Scotland has separate bands and rates.
