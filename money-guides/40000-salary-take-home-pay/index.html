£40,000 after tax: how much will you take home?

On a £40,000 salary in England, Wales or Northern Ireland, you take home about £32,320 a year, which is £2,693 a month, after Income Tax and National Insurance in the 2026/27 tax year.

HOW IT IS WORKED OUT

The £40,000 calculation, step by step.

  1. Personal Allowance: the first £12,570 is tax-free, leaving £27,430 of taxable income.
  2. Income Tax: 20% on £27,430 = £5,486.
  3. National Insurance: 8% on the £27,430 you earn between £12,570 and £50,270 = £2,194.
  4. Take-home pay: £40,000 − £5,486 − £2,194 = £32,320 a year.

You are a basic-rate taxpayer: all of your taxable income falls in the 20% basic-rate band.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

£40,000 salary FAQs

How much is £40,000 a month after tax?

About £2,693 a month in England, Wales or Northern Ireland, and £2,688 in Scotland, before any pension or student loan deductions.

What is £40,000 a year as an hourly rate?

Based on a 37.5-hour week, £40,000 is about £20.51 an hour before tax and £16.57 an hour after tax.

How much tax do I pay on £40,000?

£5,486.00 in Income Tax and £2,194.40 in National Insurance a year, a total of £7,680.40.

Why is my payslip slightly different?

Payroll works each pay period separately and rounds figures, and your tax code, benefits in kind, pension method or student loan can change the result. An emergency tax code can also cause temporary differences.
