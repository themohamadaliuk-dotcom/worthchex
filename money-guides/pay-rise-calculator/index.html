What does a pay rise really mean for your take-home pay?

A 5% or 10% pay rise changes your gross salary, but the extra money reaching your bank account depends on Income Tax, National Insurance, pension contributions and student-loan deductions.

Gross increase versus net increase

Suppose your salary rises from £40,000 by 5%. Your gross salary becomes £42,000. The increase in take-home pay will be smaller than £2,000 because the additional earnings can be subject to tax, National Insurance and other deductions.

WorthChex compares the two salaries using the same selected tax region, pension and student-loan settings so you can see an estimated monthly and annual difference.
