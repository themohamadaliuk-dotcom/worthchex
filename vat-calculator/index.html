UK VAT RATES

How this calculator works

The standard UK VAT rate is 20%. A reduced rate of 5% and a zero rate of 0% also apply to certain goods and services. Some transactions are exempt from VAT and others have special rules.

This calculator does not decide which VAT rate applies to your goods or services; it only calculates the arithmetic once you select a rate.
