QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Are the calculators really free?

Yes. Every calculator and guide is free, with no sign-up. The site is funded by advertising.

Are the figures up to date?

The tax calculators use 2026/27 rules for Income Tax, National Insurance, student loans and Stamp Duty across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Every page shows when it was last reviewed and links to the official source.

Do you store the numbers I type in?

No. Calculations run in your browser and your figures are not sent to WorthChex. See the privacy policy for how cookies and ads work.

Is this financial advice?

No. WorthChex gives estimates to help you plan and compare. For big decisions, check with the official source or a regulated adviser.
