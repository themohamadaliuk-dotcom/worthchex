2026/27 UK RULES

Gross salary is only the starting point.

The standard Personal Allowance is £12,570. Income Tax rates differ between England, Wales, Northern Ireland and Scotland, and most employees pay National Insurance between the Primary Threshold and Upper Earnings Limit, then a lower rate above it. Student-loan thresholds also vary by plan.
