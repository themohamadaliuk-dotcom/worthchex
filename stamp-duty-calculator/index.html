2026 SDLT RULES

How this calculator works

From 1 April 2025, standard residential SDLT rates are 0% to £125,000, 2% from £125,001 to £250,000, 5% from £250,001 to £925,000, 10% from £925,001 to £1.5 million and 12% above £1.5 million. Qualifying first-time buyers pay 0% on the first £300,000 and 5% on the remainder to £500,000. Higher rates can add 5 percentage points for additional dwellings, while the non-UK-resident surcharge adds 2 percentage points.

This tool does not cover every specialist relief, linked transaction, company purchase, lease transaction, shared ownership or multiple-dwellings scenario.

HOW SDLT IS CHARGED

Stamp Duty is progressive across the bands.

For a standard residential purchase, the SDLT calculation applies the relevant rate to the portion of the property price that sits within each band. That means a higher rate on the top band does not mean the whole property is taxed at that rate.

Standard purchase

The calculator applies the published residential SDLT bands for England and Northern Ireland to the price you enter.

First-time buyers

Qualifying first-time buyers can receive relief under the conditions published by GOV.UK. The property price limit matters, so check the eligibility details rather than relying on the label alone.

Additional property

Higher rates can apply when buying an additional dwelling. The position can depend on whether a previous main residence is being replaced and other facts.

Example: on a £300,000 standard purchase, the first £125,000 falls in the 0% band, the next £125,000 is charged at 2%, and the final £50,000 is charged at 5% under the published standard rates. The total is therefore not 5% of £300,000.
SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

Check the jurisdiction before using the result.

This calculator covers residential SDLT in England and Northern Ireland. Scotland uses Land and Buildings Transaction Tax, and Wales uses Land Transaction Tax, so the SDLT result is not the right tax calculation for those properties.

Property transactions can also involve circumstances outside the simple inputs on this page, including linked transactions, corporate purchases, leasehold considerations, multiple dwellings and specific reliefs. Use the result as a first estimate and check the official guidance for the actual transaction.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Stamp Duty calculator FAQs

Is Stamp Duty paid on the whole property price at one rate?

No. Residential SDLT is normally banded, so different portions of the price can be taxed at different rates.

Can every first-time buyer use the relief?

No. Eligibility has conditions including property price and the buyer's circumstances. Check the current GOV.UK rules before relying on the result.

Does Scotland use Stamp Duty?

No. Scotland uses LBTT, while Wales uses LTT. This calculator is for England and Northern Ireland SDLT.

Why can the result differ from a solicitor's figure?

A real transaction can include facts and reliefs that this simplified calculator does not model. Your legal or tax documentation should take priority.
