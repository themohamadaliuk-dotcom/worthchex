2026 RATES

Current residential rates in each UK nation.

Property tax is charged in bands, like Income Tax: each rate applies only to the part of the price that falls inside that band. A higher top rate never applies to the whole price.

England & Northern Ireland (SDLT)

0% up to £125,000 · 2% to £250,000 · 5% to £925,000 · 10% to £1.5m · 12% above.

First-time buyers: 0% up to £300,000 and 5% to £500,000, if the price is £500,000 or less.

Additional homes: +5% on every band. Non-UK residents: +2%.

Scotland (LBTT)

0% up to £145,000 · 2% to £250,000 · 5% to £325,000 · 10% to £750,000 · 12% above.

First-time buyers: the 0% band rises to £175,000.

Additional homes: Additional Dwelling Supplement of 8% of the whole price.

Wales (LTT)

0% up to £225,000 · 6% to £400,000 · 7.5% to £750,000 · 10% to £1.5m · 12% above.

There is no first-time buyer relief in Wales.

Additional homes: higher rates from 5% to 17% apply.

Worked example: a £300,000 home

England: 0% on the first £125,000, 2% on the next £125,000 (£2,500) and 5% on the last £50,000 (£2,500) = £5,000. A first-time buyer pays £0.
Scotland: 2% on £105,000 (£2,100) + 5% on £50,000 (£2,500) = £4,600, or £4,000 for a first-time buyer.
Wales: 6% on the £75,000 above £225,000 = £4,500.

Sources: GOV.UK SDLT rates · Revenue Scotland LBTT · Welsh Revenue Authority LTT

SCOPE & LIMITATIONS

What this calculator does not cover.

The calculator models a standard residential purchase by individuals. It does not cover company purchases, shared ownership, linked transactions, mixed-use or non-residential property, multiple-dwellings purchases or leases. Joint purchases where only one buyer is a first-time buyer usually do not qualify for first-time buyer relief.

Your conveyancer or solicitor will confirm the final figure and file the return. Use this estimate to budget, then check the official guidance for your situation.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Stamp Duty calculator FAQs

Is Stamp Duty paid on the whole price at one rate?

No. Each rate applies only to the slice of the price inside that band, so the effective rate is always lower than the top band rate. The one exception is Scotland's Additional Dwelling Supplement, which is 8% of the whole price.

Who counts as a first-time buyer?

Someone who has never owned a home, in the UK or anywhere else, and who will live in the property as their main home. If you buy with someone who has owned a home before, the relief usually does not apply.

When do the additional-property rates apply?

Usually when you will own more than one residential property after the purchase and you are not replacing your main home. Purchases under £40,000 are not affected.

When do I have to pay?

In England and Northern Ireland the return and payment are due within 14 days of completion. In Scotland and Wales it is 30 days. Your solicitor normally handles this for you.

Why might my solicitor's figure be different?

Real transactions can involve reliefs, linked purchases or ownership details that a simple calculator does not model. The figure on your completion statement takes priority.
