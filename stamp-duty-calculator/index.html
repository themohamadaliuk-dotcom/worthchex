2026 SDLT RULES

How this calculator works

From 1 April 2025, standard residential SDLT rates are 0% to £125,000, 2% from £125,001 to £250,000, 5% from £250,001 to £925,000, 10% from £925,001 to £1.5 million and 12% above £1.5 million. Qualifying first-time buyers pay 0% on the first £300,000 and 5% on the remainder to £500,000. Higher rates can add 5 percentage points for additional dwellings, while the non-UK-resident surcharge adds 2 percentage points.

This tool does not cover every specialist relief, linked transaction, company purchase, lease transaction, shared ownership or multiple-dwellings scenario.
