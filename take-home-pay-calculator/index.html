How this calculation works

The standard Personal Allowance is £12,570. In England, Wales and Northern Ireland the main rates are 20%, 40% and 45%; Scotland has different bands and rates. Employee Class 1 National Insurance for most employees is 8% between the Primary Threshold and Upper Earnings Limit and 2% above that. Student-loan thresholds differ by plan.

This tool is deliberately an estimate rather than a payroll replica. It does not model every tax-code, benefit, pay-period or pension-relief scenario.
